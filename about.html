Origin
Origin & founders

Built on three decades of the craft.

H&P Building was officially founded in Colorado in 2017, building on more than 30 years of combined experience in construction, custom fabrication, architectural metalwork and project management. The company was established with a vision to combine exceptional craftsmanship, American manufacturing and personalised service — creating architectural products that are both functional and visually inspiring.

The founders saw growing demand for architectural metal that was durable and functional, yet capable of enhancing the beauty and value of a property. Rather than mass-produced solutions, H&P chose to focus on fully customised products tailored to each client and project — growing into a trusted manufacturer of privacy screens, decorative panels, pergolas, fireplaces, water features, sculptures and specialty fabrication across the United States.
