01Our commitment

H&P Building LLC is committed to making hnpbuilding.com usable by as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, magnification, speech recognition or other assistive technology.

We treat accessibility as part of building the site well, not as an afterthought, and we work to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

02What we have done

Measures currently in place on this site include:

  • semantic HTML structure with a single, logical heading order on every page;
  • a "Skip to content" link at the start of every page;
  • visible keyboard focus indicators throughout, and full keyboard operability of navigation, menus and forms;
  • text alternatives for meaningful images, and decorative images marked so assistive technology can skip them;
  • form fields with associated labels, clear required-field marking, and status messages announced to screen readers;
  • color combinations chosen to meet AA contrast ratios for body text and interface elements;
  • responsive layouts that reflow without horizontal scrolling down to 320px and support browser zoom to 200%;
  • respect for the operating system's reduced-motion preference.

03Known limitations

We review the site regularly, but some areas may still fall short:

  • Some older project photography may have brief alternative text that does not fully describe the image.
  • Downloadable CAD files (DWG/DXF) are technical drawing formats and are not accessible to screen readers by nature. If you need the information in one of our drawings or specifications in an accessible format, contact us and we will provide it.
  • Content served by third parties, such as embedded maps and social media, is outside our direct control.

We are working to address these, and we will update this statement as we do.

04Tell us about a barrier

If you encounter something on this site you cannot use, we want to hear about it — and we will help you get the information you need in the meantime.

When you contact us, it helps to include the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser and assistive technology you are using. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.
