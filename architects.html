C.03 —
Rationale
Why specify H&P

Metal cladding, specified with confidence.

Architectural metal panels are practical solutions for modern construction, offering:

01 · Design

Customizable patterns & finishes

Highly customizable perforations, patterns and finishes in Corten, aluminum and stainless steel — tuned to the elevation and the light.

02 · Performance

Structural stability

Superior mechanical stability and non-combustible properties for demanding commercial and public applications.

03 · Durability

Weather resistance

Low-maintenance finishes engineered to perform outdoors for decades, including coastal and high-exposure sites.

04 · Delivery

Efficient installation

Panels designed for efficient transport and straightforward, coordinated site installation.

05 · Support

Specification support

Shop drawings, material advice and connection details from concept through to handover.

06 · Made in USA

American manufacturing

Fabricated in premium American metals with full traceability for the project record.

Workflow

How we slot into your drawing set.

Built to schedule, delivered coordinated, with installation support and documentation.
