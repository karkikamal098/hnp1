Ask directly

Our four core metals are Corten (weathering) steel — our primary material — aluminium, stainless steel and carbon steel. We also work in copper and brass for specialty pieces. We'll recommend the right metal for your application, finish and budget.

Yes. Most of our work starts from an architect's or builder's drawings. We review the set, advise on material and detailing, produce shop drawings for your approval, and engineer the connections to code before fabrication begins.

Yes. We cut to custom perforation patterns — including bespoke patterns developed from a motif, logo or image — and finish in weathering patina, powder-coat (any colour), anodised or brushed. Physical samples are provided for approval.

Yes. CAD blocks (DWG/DXF), PDF specifications and finish references are available for your drawing set, and we provide project-specific shop drawings. Physical material and finish samples can be ordered.

Every project is quoted individually against the specification — material, size, pattern, finish, engineering and quantity. Send drawings or a description via the quote form and we'll respond with options and pricing.

Lead time depends on the system, quantity and finish, and is confirmed with your quote. We plan fabrication around your construction schedule and coordinate delivery accordingly.

Yes. We're based in Colorado and serve clients across the United States, with panels designed for efficient transport and straightforward site installation.

We fabricate and deliver, and provide installation guidance and documentation. For many projects the client's contractor installs; we support them with shop drawings and detailing so it lands right on site.
