How we work with you

Landscape architects choose H&P for elements that live outside and get better with age. Corten's weathering patina suits planting and hardscape beautifully, and needs no maintenance once stabilised. We fabricate planters, screens, edging, water and fire features to any dimension, carrying one material language across a whole scheme.
