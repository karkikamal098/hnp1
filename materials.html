Corten Steel

Weathering steel forms a stable, protective rust-like patina that removes the need for painting and deepens over time. Architects and builders choose Corten for its living finish, structural strength and exceptional outdoor durability — ideal for facades, screens, planters and sculptural elements.

Best forFacades, screens, planters, sculpture
FinishNatural weathering patina / sealed
MaintenanceEffectively zero once stabilised
LifespanDecades in exterior applications

Aluminium

Corrosion-resistant and light without sacrificing rigidity, aluminium is ideal where weight, large spans or a wide colour range matter. It accepts powder-coat and anodised finishes across the full spectrum, making it a favourite for perforated facade systems and screening.

Best forPerforated facades, screens, large panels
FinishPowder-coat, anodised, mill
Weight~⅓ the weight of steel
MaintenanceLow — naturally corrosion-resistant

Where a crisp, contemporary finish and long service life are essential, stainless delivers. Its strength and corrosion resistance make it the standard for railings, guardrails and stair systems in high-traffic commercial and hospitality settings.
