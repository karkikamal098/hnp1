01Who we are

H&P Building LLC ("H&P Building," "we," "us," or "our") is a custom architectural metal fabricator based in Hartsel, Colorado. We design, engineer and fabricate made-to-order metalwork for architects, builders, developers and property owners across the United States.

This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website, through quote requests, and through our correspondence with you. It applies to hnpbuilding.com and to the inquiries we receive from it.

02Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and to run our business.

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, company or practice name, email address and phone number.
  • Project information — project name or reference, requested material, drawings, specifications, dimensions, finishes, load cases, site details and timelines that you send us.
  • Correspondence — the content of emails, form submissions and phone conversations relating to your project.
  • Order and billing information — if you place an order, the details needed to invoice, fulfill and deliver it, including delivery address and purchase-order references.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files used to keep the site working and to understand how it is used. See Cookies and analytics.

We do not ask for, and ask that you do not send us, sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, health information or financial account credentials. Payment card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored on our systems.

03How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • respond to quote requests and prepare pricing, material options and feasibility advice;
  • produce shop drawings, engineer connections and fabricate your order;
  • coordinate delivery, freight and installation documentation;
  • invoice you and keep the accounting and tax records we are required to keep;
  • provide customer support, warranty assistance and after-sales service;
  • send project updates and, where you have asked for them, occasional updates about our work;
  • maintain the security and performance of this website and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • comply with our legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

04Cookies and analytics

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to load, display correctly and keep your session secure. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics — aggregated measurement of how the site is used, so we can improve navigation and content. This data is reported to us in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally.

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the site from working. Where your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid opt-out request under applicable state law.

05How we share information

We share personal information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers — hosting, email, analytics, e-commerce, payment processing and freight carriers who process information on our behalf and are bound to use it only for the services they provide to us.
  • Freight and delivery partners — the name, delivery address and phone number needed to complete your delivery.
  • Professional advisers — accountants, insurers and lawyers, where necessary and under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Legal and safety — where we are required to by law, subpoena or court order, or where disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of others.
  • Business transfers — if H&P Building is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any material change to how your information is handled.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

06How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described above.

  • Quote requests that do not become orders — generally up to 24 months, so we can pick up a conversation if your project resumes.
  • Project and order records, including drawings — retained for the life of the product and as long as needed to support warranty, engineering and liability obligations.
  • Accounting and tax records — retained for the period required by federal and Colorado law.
  • Website analytics — retained in aggregate form.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or render it permanently de-identified.

07How we protect it

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold. This site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, access to project and customer records is limited to personnel who need it, and our service providers are selected on the basis of their security practices.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

08Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and obtain a copy of it.
  • Correct — ask us to fix inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete — ask us to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and contractual exceptions.
  • Portability — receive your information in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Opt out — opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, or profiling with legal effects. As noted above, we do not sell personal information or engage in cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-discrimination — exercise these rights without being denied service or given a different level of service.

Residents of Colorado have these rights under the Colorado Privacy Act, and residents of California have comparable rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA. Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws have similar rights. We extend the core rights above to all our customers regardless of where they live.

To exercise a right, email sales@hnpbuilding.com or write to us at the address below. We will verify your identity before acting on the request — usually by confirming details already associated with your project or order — and will respond within 45 days, extendable once where permitted by law. You may use an authorized agent, and if we decline a request you may appeal by replying to our decision; we will respond to an appeal within 45 days.

You can unsubscribe from any marketing email at any time using the link in the message, or by contacting us directly. We will still send transactional messages about an active order.

09Children's privacy

Our website and services are directed to construction, design and property professionals and to adult customers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and our services are not intended for them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Third-party links and services

This site links to third-party sites, including our online store, social media profiles and mapping services. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices. We encourage you to read the policy of any site you visit from ours.

11Visitors outside the United States

We are based in the United States and our systems and service providers are located here. If you contact us from outside the United States, your personal information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection law may differ from that of your own country. By sending us an inquiry you consent to that transfer.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, our services or the law. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
