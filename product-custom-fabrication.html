Overview

Built for the specification.

Everything else on this site started as a custom job. If you can draw it, we can most likely build it — in American steel, stainless, aluminum, copper, brass or corten. Gates are a mainstay of this system: solid Corten driveway swing gates, horizontal-slat pedestrian gates in aluminum or steel with electronic-lock compatibility, intricately laser-cut sliding or double-swing statement gates, and freestanding garden rings that double as a moongate or event backdrop. Bring a sketch, an elevation or a full drawing set; we'll advise on material and feasibility, produce shop drawings, engineer the connections and fabricate to tight tolerances. This is the heart of what H&P does.
