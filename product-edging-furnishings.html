Overview

Built for the specification.

The small elements of a landscape carry a lot of wear and a lot of the detail. We fabricate edging, tree grates, bollards, benches and site furnishings in Corten and aluminium — durable, coordinated and built to public-realm standards. A single material palette across these pieces gives a scheme a considered, unified feel.
