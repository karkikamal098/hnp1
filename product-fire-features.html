Overview

Built for the specification.

A fire feature draws people to the center of an outdoor space. We fabricate fire pits, bowls and tables in Corten and steel, from compact 24" wood-burning patio bowls to 60"-wide gas installations, plus a 43" nesting kit designed to ship flat, and a wheeled wood-storage combo unit for hospitality use. Gas models can run on natural gas or propane with a choice of ignition, and a matte-black suspended gas fireplace extends the range indoors. The weathering finish suits landscape and hospitality schemes, ageing into the surroundings rather than standing apart from them.

Before you specify

Common questions.

Both. Our wood-burning bowls and pits range from a compact 24" patio bowl to a 43" nesting kit, while the VULCANO gas range runs on natural gas or propane with electronic, flame-sense or match-lit ignition.

Standard sizes run from 24" wide or diameter up to 60" wide on the gas range, with a 43" round nesting kit in between. Custom dimensions are available across nearly every model.

Yes — our suspended hanging fireplace is a matte black steel unit for interiors, available in 28", 36" and 39" sizes plus custom, running on natural gas or propane. It requires professional installation and a flue.

Fire pits are fabricated in 3mm Corten weathering steel and supplied raw or pre-oxidized. The rust patina deepens over the first months and then stabilizes, so no painting or ongoing finishing is required.

The HNP-FP071 pairs a removable fire tray with an integrated firewood store on heavy-duty lockable castors, so it can be repositioned across a terrace and doubles as a grilling station — well suited to hotels, restaurants and rooftops.

Very little. Clear ash between uses, keep the drain clear so water does not sit in the bowl, and allow the patina to do its work. Gas units are supplied pre-assembled and are essentially maintenance-free.
