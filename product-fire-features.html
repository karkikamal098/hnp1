Overview

Built for the specification.

A fire feature draws people to the centre of an outdoor space. We fabricate fire pits, bowls and tables in Corten and steel, sized to the setting and detailed for gas or wood. The weathering finish suits landscape and hospitality schemes, ageing into the surroundings rather than standing apart from them.
