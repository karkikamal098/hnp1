Overview

Built for the specification.

Planters define the edges of a landscape and carry a project's material language into the greenery. We fabricate them to any dimension and profile in Corten or aluminum, with drainage and liner detailing built in — from single boxes and raised beds (nine preset sizes from 24"×12"×12" up to 36"×24"×24", plus custom) to trellis-integrated planters with a built-in lattice for climbing vines, and root-friendly tree grates for streetscape planting. Corten's warm patina complements planting beautifully; aluminum offers a lighter unit and a full color range for rooftop weight limits.

Drainage is built in — most raised beds use an open-bottom flat-panel design that drains directly to the ground, and closed planters are detailed with drainage holes and liner provision appropriate to the planting.
