Privacy Screens

Decorative screening systems that deliver privacy while preserving airflow, light and architectural intent.

Corten SteelAluminumStainless
Request a quote →Specs & CAD
Overview

Built for the specification.

Our privacy screens turn a functional requirement into a design feature. Laser-cut or perforated to a custom pattern — from botanical florals to a redwood-forest silhouette — they shield balconies, courtyards, pool areas and service zones without closing a space off. Panels typically run 24"–60" wide and 48"–96" tall, sold single or as multi-panel sets, with a dedicated enclosure format built to screen pool pumps, filters and heaters behind a ventilated service gate. In weathering Corten they develop a warm, living patina; in powder-coated aluminum or marine-grade stainless they hold a clean contemporary finish. Each screen is engineered for wind load and fixed with concealed brackets, so the pattern reads clean from both sides.

Questions
Before you specify

Common questions.

Panels typically run 24"–60" wide and 48"–96" tall, supplied singly or as coordinated multi-panel sets. Any dimension outside that range is made to order — send us the opening and we will size the panels and posts to suit.

Corten weathering steel is the most popular choice for its living rust patina. We also fabricate in powder-coated aluminum for a full color range, and in 304 or 316 marine-grade stainless where coastal exposure or a crisp contemporary finish is required.

While the patina is stabilizing, rain can carry rust-colored runoff onto porous surfaces such as light concrete, render or natural stone. We detail drainage and stand-offs to manage this, and can supply panels pre-oxidized so most of the initial run-off has already happened before delivery.

Yes. Alongside our stock designs — botanical florals, coast redwood forest, bamboo, wildlife scenes and organic bubble perforation — we laser-cut custom patterns developed from a motif, logo, photograph or an architect’s artwork. Samples are provided for approval before production.

Depending on the setting: stand-off screws or pins to a wall, concealed brackets, posts set into footings, or a freestanding welded-post frame. Every screen is engineered for the wind load at its location so the pattern reads clean from both sides.

Yes. We build a dedicated enclosure format — a freestanding welded-post system with a double service gate that swings clear — to screen pumps, filters, heaters and plumbing while the perforation keeps airflow to the equipment.
