Privacy Screens

Decorative screening systems that deliver privacy while preserving airflow, light and architectural intent.

Corten SteelAluminiumStainless
Request a quote →Specs & CAD
Overview

Built for the specification.

Our privacy screens turn a functional requirement into a design feature. Laser-cut or perforated to a custom pattern, they shield balconies, courtyards, pool areas and service zones without closing a space off. In weathering Corten they develop a warm, living patina; in powder-coated aluminium they hold any colour in the spectrum. Each screen is engineered for wind load and fixed with concealed brackets, so the pattern reads clean from both sides.
