Overview

Built for the specification.

A staircase is a sculptural moment as much as a circulation route. We fabricate stair railings to the exact rake, radius and dimensions of your stair, with continuous handrails, engineered connections and a finish that reads seamless from bottom to top. Straight, curved or switchback — the geometry is drawn from your stair drawings and delivered ready to fix. For outdoor sites we also build full weathering-steel garden stairs — configurable from 2 to 7 steps, 36"–72" wide, with single or double railings — and offer decorative copper-look riser cladding in bronze, aged copper noir and verdigris finishes for refreshing existing treads.
