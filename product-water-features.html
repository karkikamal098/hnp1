Overview

Built for the specification.

Water and weathering steel are a natural pairing — the patina deepens where water runs, and the sound anchors a space. We fabricate water walls, spill bowls, runnels and fountains in Corten, copper or stainless, engineered for clean sheeting and reliable circulation. Each feature is built as a signature element for a plaza, entry court or private garden, and detailed to age gracefully outdoors.
