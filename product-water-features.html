Overview

Built for the specification.

Water and weathering steel are a natural pairing — the patina deepens where water runs, and the sound anchors a space. The range spans wall-mounted cascades, sculptural fountain bowls and arc-form spillways in Corten or polished stainless, typically 48"–78" wide and up to 96" tall, with custom dimensions available on every model. Every fountain ships with a concealed pump and plumbing, and select designs add an auto-fill system or integrated LED lighting; for a fuller outdoor-living statement we also fabricate a compact above-ground plunge pool with optional heating, saltwater sanitization and smart controls. Each feature is built as a signature element for a plaza, entry court or private garden, and detailed to age gracefully outdoors.

Questions
Before you specify

Common questions.

Standard panel and basin combinations generally run from 48" to 78" wide and up to 96" tall, with fountain bowls from around 28" to 54" in diameter. Custom dimensions are available on every model in the range.

Yes. Every water feature ships with a concealed, commercial-grade pump and hidden plumbing so no equipment is visible in the finished installation. An automatic top-up (auto-fill) system is available as an option.

Yes — integrated LED lighting can be built into the spillway or basin on most designs, which is what gives the sheet of water its glow after dark. Specify it at quote stage so the housings are fabricated in.

Corten weathering steel is the signature material, with polished or 304 stainless steel for a sleek reflective look and copper for specialty pieces. Stainless spillways and basin inserts can be combined with a Corten body.

In climates with hard frost the feature should be drained down and the pump removed for winter. The steel itself is unaffected by cold — this is about protecting the pump and plumbing from ice.

Yes. Our above-ground plunge pool has a powder-coated steel shell with an insulated fibreglass interior, needs no excavation, and offers optional heating, saltwater or UV sanitization, LED lighting, a cover, a built-in bench and smart controls.
