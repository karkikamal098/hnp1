01The short version

Nearly everything we make is fabricated to your specification, so it cannot be resold. Orders may be modified or canceled only before production begins. Once production starts, custom orders cannot be canceled, returned or refunded — except where there is a manufacturing defect or shipping damage, which we will put right.

Inspect your delivery on arrival and report any damage within 48 hours.

02Custom and made-to-order products

H&P Building manufactures architectural metalwork to order. Each piece is cut, formed, finished and engineered to the dimensions, pattern, finish and load case agreed for your project. It has no resale value to another customer.

For that reason:

  • Orders may be modified or canceled only before production begins.
  • Once production has begun, custom orders cannot be canceled, returned or refunded, except for manufacturing defects or shipping damage as set out below.
  • Approval of shop drawings is the point at which the specification is fixed. Please review dimensions, materials, mounting details and finish carefully before approving.

03Cancellation before production

To cancel or change an order, contact us in writing as early as possible at sales@hnpbuilding.com with your order number.

  • If production has not begun, we will cancel the order and refund amounts paid, less any engineering, drafting, material procurement or restocking costs already incurred on your behalf. Where no such costs have been incurred, the refund is in full.
  • If production has begun, cancellation is generally not possible. We will tell you honestly what stage your order has reached and what, if anything, can be done.

04Manufacturing defects

If a product arrives with a manufacturing defect — a fault in materials or workmanship — we will put it right.

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • your order number;
  • clear photographs showing the issue, and the packaging if relevant;
  • a description of the problem.

We will review the claim and, at our option, repair the item, replace it, or refund the amount paid for it. We may ask you to return the item, or a sample of it, for inspection; where a defect is confirmed, return freight is at our cost.

05Shipping damage

Risk of loss passes on delivery to the carrier, so prompt inspection protects both of us and preserves the freight claim.

  1. Inspect before signing. Where damage is visible, note it in detail on the carrier's delivery receipt before you sign. "Subject to inspection" alone is not sufficient.
  2. Photograph everything — the crate or packaging as delivered, and the goods.
  3. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number, photographs and a description.

We will handle the carrier claim and arrange repair, replacement or refund. Failure to note damage on the delivery receipt, or to report it within 48 hours, may prevent recovery against the carrier and may invalidate the claim.

06What is not a defect

Corten steel, copper, brass and other reactive metals are chosen precisely because they change over time.

Variations in color, texture, oxidation, weathering and patina are natural characteristics of these materials and are not defects. Patina develops over months and varies with orientation, shelter, rainfall and local atmosphere. Panels weather differently depending on where they sit, and adjacent pieces will not match exactly. Runoff staining on porous adjacent surfaces during early weathering is a characteristic of the material, not a fault in the product.

Minor variation in finish, mill marks inherent to the material, and normal tolerances of fabrication are likewise not defects.

07After delivery

H&P Building is not responsible for damage resulting from improper handling, installation, modification, misuse or improper maintenance following delivery.

This includes damage caused during unloading or site storage, cutting or drilling after delivery, installation to an inadequate substrate, refinishing by others, and exposure to de-icing salts, pool or marine chemicals, or other aggressive conditions not disclosed to us at quotation stage.

08Production and delivery delays

Production and delivery timelines are estimates. Delays arising from material availability, supplier or finishing schedules, weather, or carrier scheduling do not qualify an order for cancellation or refund. We will keep you updated and work to recover schedule wherever we can.

09How approved refunds are issued

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once we process a refund, the time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer — typically 5–10 business days. We will confirm by email when a refund has been issued.

Where a refund is partial, we will set out clearly what has been refunded and what has been retained, and why.

10How to make a claim

Email sales@hnpbuilding.com or call (720) 609-9307 with your order number, photographs and a description of the issue. Claims relating to delivery must reach us within 48 hours of delivery.

We aim to acknowledge every claim within 1–2 business days and to tell you the outcome, or what we need next, promptly after that.
