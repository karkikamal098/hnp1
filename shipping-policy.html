01Production lead times

Production typically takes approximately 6–10 weeks, measured from the latest of:

  • confirmation of your order;
  • receipt of your deposit; and
  • your written approval of shop drawings.

Highly customized pieces, large commercial packages and specialty finishes may require additional time; where that applies, your quote will say so. Lead time is confirmed with your quote, and we plan fabrication around your construction schedule wherever you give us the dates.

Production and delivery dates are estimates. Material availability, supplier and finishing schedules, weather and carrier scheduling can affect them, and we will keep you informed if a date moves.

02How we ship

The method depends on the size, weight and dimensions of what we have made:

  • Smaller items ship via UPS or FedEx.
  • Large and oversized products — screens, facade packages, railings, planters, water and fire features — ship by LTL freight carrier or dedicated delivery service.

Work is crated or palletized appropriately for transport, and panels are designed for efficient transport and straightforward site handling. We will confirm the carrier and provide tracking or a delivery appointment reference once your order ships.

03Where we ship

We ship throughout the United States from our facility in Hartsel, Colorado, and serve architects, builders, developers and property owners nationwide.

For destinations outside the United States, contact us before ordering so we can confirm availability, freight cost, and any duties, taxes or documentation involved. International duties and import charges are the responsibility of the recipient.

04Shipping costs

Freight is quoted per project. Rates depend on the size, weight and dimensions of the goods, the destination, and the delivery method required.

Additional charges may apply for:

  • liftgate service;
  • residential delivery;
  • limited-access, rural or remote destinations;
  • inside delivery, special handling or dedicated transport;
  • redelivery or storage where a delivery cannot be completed.

Tell us the delivery address and any site access constraints at quotation stage and we will include the correct freight service in your quote, so there are no surprises later.

05Delivery day and unloading

Most large products are delivered curbside. The carrier brings the shipment to the nearest accessible point at the address and is not responsible for moving it onto the site, into a building, or around obstacles.

Before delivery, please make sure that:

  • the delivery location is accessible to a full-size freight vehicle;
  • someone is present to receive, inspect and sign for the shipment;
  • you have arranged the equipment and labor needed to unload — forklift, telehandler, crane or sufficient personnel, depending on the size and weight of the pieces;
  • you have somewhere dry and level to store the goods until installation.

Unloading is the customer's responsibility unless your quote expressly includes it. If a delivery cannot be completed because the site is inaccessible or nobody is available to receive it, redelivery and storage charges are your responsibility.

06Inspect your shipment on arrival

This is the most important step on delivery day, and it protects your claim.

  1. Inspect the shipment immediately, before the carrier leaves.
  2. Note any visible damage in detail on the delivery receipt before signing.
  3. Photograph the crate or packaging as delivered, and the goods.
  4. Contact H&P Building within 48 hours of delivery with your order number, photographs and a description of the issue.

Failure to note damage on the delivery receipt, or to report it within 48 hours, may prevent recovery against the carrier and may invalidate the claim. Full details are in our Refund Policy.

07Title and risk of loss

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, shipment is Ex Works / FOB Origin, and title and risk of loss pass to you when the goods are delivered to the carrier. The inspection and 48-hour reporting process above is how damage in transit is resolved.

08Installation

We fabricate and deliver. We provide installation guidance, shop drawings and documentation, and we support your contractor throughout — but installation itself is carried out by your installer unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. See our Terms of Service for the full scope.
