13Finishes and natural weathering

Corten and other weathering steels are designed to oxidize. The patina develops over months, deepens with exposure, and varies with orientation, shelter, rainfall and local atmosphere. Copper, brass and unlacquered finishes behave similarly. Adjacent panels, and panels installed at different times, will not weather identically.

Variation in color, texture, oxidation, weathering and patina is a natural characteristic of these materials and is not a defect. During the early weathering period, runoff from weathering steel can stain porous adjacent surfaces such as concrete, stone and paving. Detailing to manage runoff is the responsibility of the designer and installer; we are glad to advise at design stage.
